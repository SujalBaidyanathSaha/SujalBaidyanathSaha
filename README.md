## 📂 Featured Projects

### 📊 [Indian Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis](https://github.com/SujalBaidyanathSaha/Indian-Retail-Sales-Profitability-Analysis-Project)
`Excel` `PostgreSQL` `Python (Pandas)` `Power BI`

An end-to-end analysis of **100,000 retail transactions (2019–2023)**, built to understand not just how much a business sells but how much it actually earns.

| Sales | Profit | Margin | Avg. Discount |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Rs. 250.84 Cr** | **Rs. 37.55 Cr** | **14.97%** | **25.13%** |

- Cleaned and validated a public Kaggle dataset, then analysed sales, profit and margin by category, state, region, segment and discount band using SQL
- **Key finding:** profit margin fell from **19.05%** (0–10% discount) to **11.09%** (40–50% discount), noted as an association rather than proof of cause
- Built a **3-page interactive Power BI dashboard**: Executive Overview, Product & Market Performance, Profitability & Discount Analysis

### 💰 [Budget vs Actual Financial Performance Dashboard](https://github.com/SujalBaidyanathSaha/Budget-vs-Actual-Financial-Performance-Project)
`Excel` `Power BI` `DAX` `Pivot Tables`

A reusable variance-analysis dashboard built on a **simulated dataset** (2,304 AI-generated records) to practise financial performance monitoring.

- Created **DAX measures** for Variance and Variance %, with a **4-page Power BI report**: Executive, Department, Region and Expense views
- On the simulated data it surfaces a Rs. 1 Cr (1.64%) overspend led by Operations and Marketing; these figures are illustrative, not real company results
